# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Windows desktop (WPF).

## Users

SES Energy service technicians and engineers, including personnel working on a helideck.

## Product Purpose

MVS enables engineers to verify, document, and demonstrate the accuracy of helideck motion monitoring systems so operational decisions can be based on trustworthy motion data.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve existing features, workflows, and content during visual redesign. Changes to functionality require user approval.

## Brand Commitments

Use the visual language of the Helideck Inspection Platform, adapted to MVS without copying its functionality or content.
